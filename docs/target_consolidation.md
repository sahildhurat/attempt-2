# Proposed Target Taxonomy Consolidation

## target

**[Specific Person]**: Photos of a particular named individual, searchable by face recognition, name, or person tags.
*Merged from*: specific_person, specific_person_face_group, self_portraits
*Example phrases*: Photos of the user's daughter; Photos of a specific friend named Don Noo; Photos of the user themselves (self-portraits)

**[Multiple People Together]**: Photos containing two or more specific named people or the user with another person in the same image.
*Merged from*: multiple_people_together, multiple_specific_people
*Example phrases*: A photo of the user and his son; Photos containing both Jack and Jill together; Photos showing both the user and their partner together

**[Pet or Animal]**: Photos of specific named pets or animals, searchable by pet name or pet face recognition.
*Merged from*: specific_pet, pet_photos, pet_or_animal
*Example phrases*: Photos of a specific dog named Roscoe; All photos of a pet rabbit named Pebby; Photos of the user's new cat (a dark tortie)

**[Specific Date]**: Photos taken on a particular date or recurring dates across multiple years.
*Merged from*: specific_date_or_time, specific_date
*Example phrases*: Photos taken on August 26, 2024; Photos from December 23rd across different years; Photos taken between 8:10AM and 8:20AM over a 2-year period

**[Date Range or Period]**: Photos from a specific month, year, or extended time period.
*Merged from*: specific_year, date_range_or_period
*Example phrases*: All photos from 2023; Photos from February 2020; Photos from a specific date range (8/20/25 to 10/1/25)

**[Location or Place]**: Photos taken at a particular geographic location, place, or address, including geotagged photos.
*Merged from*: specific_location, location_or_place
*Example phrases*: Photos taken at the user's cabin; Photos from a trip to Thailand; Photos taken at Havelock Island

**[Event or Occasion]**: Photos from a particular event, occasion, vacation, wedding, birthday, or trip.
*Merged from*: specific_event, event_or_occasion, event
*Example phrases*: Photos from a wedding on 28th August 2021; Photos from a walk around Seattle taken 10+ years ago; Photos from a friend's birthday party

**[Object or Category]**: Photos containing a specific object, animal type, or general visual category searchable by keyword or visual recognition.
*Merged from*: object_or_subject_content, object_category_search, object_or_category
*Example phrases*: Photos showing red cars; Photos of dogs; Photos of Christmas trees

**[Document or Text Content]**: Photos of documents, receipts, IDs, or images with specific visible text or numbers searchable via OCR or manual description.
*Merged from*: text_or_document, text_in_image_search, document_or_receipt, document_or_text_content
*Example phrases*: A photo of a piece of paper showing a WiFi password; Photos containing specific text (e.g., ID card numbers); Screenshots containing specific words

**[Filename or Identifier]**: Photos located by their specific filename, filename pattern, or technical timestamp identifier.
*Merged from*: file_identifier, file_name_search, filename_or_identifier
*Example phrases*: A specific photo file with filename '20211204_172629'; Photos being searched for by their file name; A photo identified by filename-style timestamp

**[Custom Description or Caption]**: Photos found by searching for manually added captions, descriptions, custom tags, or keywords in photo metadata.
*Merged from*: photo_description_caption_search, custom_description_or_tag
*Example phrases*: A photo the user had added a specific caption to; Photos tagged with keywords in photo descriptions; Photos found by caption text or metadata labels

**[Device or Camera Source]**: Photos taken with or synced from a particular camera, phone, or device, searchable by device metadata.
*Merged from*: specific_device_or_camera, device_or_camera_source, device_or_camera
*Example phrases*: Photos taken with specific camera models (e.g., EOS 90D, Sony camera); Photos from a specific person's device; Photos taken by a particular device

**[Album or Collection]**: Photos organized within a specific named album, folder, or collection created by the user or shared.
*Merged from*: album_or_collection
*Example phrases*: A specific album titled 'Home Delivery nov 24'; Previously created albums in Google Photos; An album the user created with 'Mayatan' in the title

**[Face Group or People Feature]**: Photos organized by Google Photos' People & Pets face recognition and grouping feature.
*Merged from*: face_grouping_or_people, face_grouping_issue, face_grouping_or_people_feature
*Example phrases*: Previously identified face groups/people in Google Photos; Photos of a person split into two separate person-groups; Face groups that were accidentally merged

**[Shared Content]**: Photos shared by others with the user, or photos the user shared, including partner sharing and shared albums.
*Merged from*: shared_or_received, shared_album_photos, shared_or_collaborative_album, partner_sharing, shared_content
*Example phrases*: Photos shared by a friend in a shared album; Photos uploaded by a friend to a shared album; Photos from spouse's Google Photos account via partner sharing

**[Hidden or Locked Content]**: Photos stored in the Locked Folder, hidden, archived, or otherwise marked as private or inaccessible in Google Photos.
*Merged from*: locked_or_hidden, hidden_or_locked_content
*Example phrases*: Photos stored in the Locked Folder in Google Photos; Previously hidden photos the user wants to unhide; A person face group that the user had hidden

**[Edited or Modified Photo]**: Photos that have been edited, annotated, or saved as copies with modifications.
*Merged from*: edited_or_modified, edited_or_modified_copy, edited_or_modified_photo
*Example phrases*: Edited photos saved as a copy via 'Save a Copy'; An edited photo (edited version of an original image file); Photos edited with Magic Eraser

**[Upload or Backup Status]**: Photos identified by when they were uploaded, backed up, or synced to Google Photos, rather than when they were originally taken.
*Merged from*: backup_or_synced, recently_added_or_uploaded, upload_or_backup_time
*Example phrases*: Photos previously backed up and then removed from device storage; Photos backed up on a particular day; Recently uploaded photos

**[File Type or Format]**: Photos filtered by technical file type, format, or media characteristics (RAW, DNG, HDR, motion photos, videos, screenshots, etc.).
*Merged from*: motion_or_video, raw_or_format_specific, raw_or_special_format, file_or_media_type
*Example phrases*: All RAW photo files with .dng extension; Motion photos or Live Photos; All of the user's HDR (High Dynamic Range) photos

**[Video or Motion Content]**: Videos, motion photos, live photos, or specific frames/stills extracted from motion sequences.
*Merged from*: motion_photo_frame, video_specific, motion_photo_or_live_photo
*Example phrases*: A specific still frame from a motion photo; Videos stored in Google Photos; Burst photos taken on an iPhone and stored in Google Photos

**[Screenshot or System File]**: Screenshots, screen recordings, or files stored in system folders like Screenshots or WhatsApp.
*Merged from*: screenshot_or_system_file, whatsapp_or_messaging
*Example phrases*: Screenshots saved in the device's screenshots folder; A screenshot containing a specific word; WhatsApp images with a specific file name format

**[Metadata or Technical Issue]**: Photos with incorrect, mismatched, or corrupted metadata, date information, sorting problems, or missing sync status.
*Merged from*: metadata_or_property, backed_up_or_synced_photos, synced_or_backup_status
*Example phrases*: Photos with incorrect EXIF/metadata capture dates; Pictures with older dates that stay at the top instead of sorting below recent photos; Photos transferred from old phone, now jumbled instead of organized by date

**[Missing or Lost Content]**: Photos or albums that previously existed but are no longer visible, accessible, or syncing in Google Photos.
*Merged from*: missing_or_inaccessible, backed_up_or_synced_photos, missing_or_lost, missing_or_lost_content
*Example phrases*: A missing photo that the user could not locate in Google Photos; Photos from February 2020 that are missing from the main library; 1000 missing photos from the user's Google account

**[Memory or Auto-Generated Feature]**: Photos or content appearing in Google Photos' Memories, highlights, Year-in-review, Moments, or other automatically created compilations.
*Merged from*: auto_generated_content, memory_feature_content, memory_or_auto_generated, feature_or_view_content
*Example phrases*: Specific photos appearing in Google Photos 'Memories' feature; Auto-generated compilation/highlight videos; 2025 Year-in-review 'recap' feature

**[Face Recognition Error]**: Photos that were incorrectly grouped, mislabeled, merged, split, or hidden under the wrong person's face group in Google Photos.
*Merged from*: face_grouping_issue, face_recognition_error
*Example phrases*: Photos of the user's youngest son merged with photos of another son; Photos of a specific person split into two separate person-groups; Photos of a new cat misidentified with an old deceased cat

**[Reverse Image Search]**: A specific photo located via reverse image search or visual search functionality within Google Photos.
*Merged from*: reverse_image_search
*Example phrases*: A specific photo selected by the user attempted via reverse-image-search; Photos found through visual search matching

**[Duplicate or Redundant]**: Duplicate, near-duplicate, or redundant photos within the library.
*Merged from*: duplicate_or_redundant
*Example phrases*: Duplicate photos within Google Photos library; Edited copies of original photos with special filename patterns; Thumbnail photos created by Picasa app

**[Quality or Storage Attribute]**: Photos identified by storage quality, compression status, or storage quota impact.
*Merged from*: quality_or_storage_attribute
*Example phrases*: All photos stored in 'original quality'; Photos that need compression via 'recover space'

**[Search or Feature Functionality Issue]**: Issues related to Google Photos search features, filters, sorting, or functionality rather than finding specific photo content.
*Merged from*: feature_or_functionality, search_or_sorting_issue
*Example phrases*: The old/classic Google Photos search feature; Photos failing to appear in keyword search results; Motion photos category/search filter functionality

**[Unspecified or Unknown]**: Cases where the specific photo target is not clearly described, insufficient information to categorize, or discussed tangentially.
*Merged from*: unknown, unspecified_or_unknown, unknown_or_unspecified
*Example phrases*: UNKNOWN - no specific user search target described; Unspecified photos; User describes general search experience
