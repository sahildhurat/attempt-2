COMMENT 4 (PS3ForTheLoss, 7d ago):
If you shot the photos with your phone, there is a chance that they may be in its storage.

Try checking the Files app. Go to /storage/emulated/0/Pictures.
Or if you are an iPhone user, they should be apparent but if not you can try accessing the local Photos app to see if any remains are present.

Given what you've said, I don't think the images are going to be found here (Android/iOS).

You CAN try to contact Google about recovering photos. They'll probably give you what this sub generally does that you seem to know of as far as the comments/post herein.
Other question: did you have a Family Group set up before the photos deleted? If so, there is a slim chance that any "family" who may have been added onto your Family Group could have devices who had access to your past images.
For example, if your family member added to the account (Jim, Jack, Jessica ...) was logged into Google Photos and had your images pulled up at the time -- and the noted device has been offline since the specified time -- they would technically be able to see any and all pictures you had on Google Photos to download et al. as they (or you, if you had access to the noted device) please.
I take it you did not make a takeout file that happened to have every photo on it before this (possible) loss?

COMMENT 6 (h_grytpype_thynne, 7d ago):
I'm sorry that all you can get here is lessons for the future.
Never ever ever share accounts or login credentials with anyone, ever.

Always keep backups of anything you don't want to lose. A good backup plan is to always have at least three copies of the data on at least two different kinds of storage (like local and cloud) with at least one in a separate location (cloud again). For photos, you can use Takeout, or just download them in batches.
