Hi Drx Ahmad Raza The photos (and videos) are deleted from the trash after 60 days, everything that is deleted from the trash, either because it is emptied manually or because this time has passed, is permanently deleted and it is impossible to recover it. You have more information in the following link: Delete or restore your photos & videos Also, in the following thread of this community you can find all the information and related help to recover photos and videos: Restore your recently deleted photos & videos Remember, Google cannot recover photos and videos you have deleted for you , regardless of how important they are.
