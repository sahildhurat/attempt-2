What the walkthrough covers

  1. The runnable workflow — a report from the corpus through the relevance gate, grounded extraction and taxonomy assignment, with each stage's real prompt.
  2. What people were looking for, and how old the photos were — 69% of the units that stated an age were old photos.
  3. What they remembered and what they had lost — content description leads at 140 of 554 cues; only 26 of the 61 forgotten items were genuine memory gaps.
  4. How they searched, and where it broke — 60% of quoted queries were one word; text and keyword search is the largest failure at 37 of 164.
  5. How it was built and checked — every pass blind-tested, with the weak scores published alongside the strong ones.
  6. The incomplete-memory segment — the 51 units that held one cue and had lost another, and the 21 that still held a description.
  7. The opportunity, and the limitations — including what a complaint corpus cannot tell you.
