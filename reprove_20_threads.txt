[REPLY 1 | Author: LeadMc Music Artist | Badge: 0 | Parent: 467767648]
Hi, If the photos stopped loading after you moved them to the SD card, the first thing I would check is whether the originals were already backed up to Google Photos. Open Google Photos and check the same photos at photos.google.com . If they display correctly there, your backed-up copies should be safe and you can download them again. If they are not backed up, avoid moving, deleting, formatting, or adding anything else to the SD card for now. Try inserting the card into another device or computer and see if the image files can be opened or copied from there. Clearing the Google Photos cache will not repair a corrupted file. If the actual files on the SD card were damaged during the transfer, recovery may need to be attempted from the card itself. Also check a few affected files in another gallery or file manager app. That will help determine whether the files themselves are damaged or Google Photos is simply having trouble accessing them. Kind regards, Kevin
